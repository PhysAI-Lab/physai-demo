Principal investigator

Peter Yichen Chen

Assistant Professor · UBC Computer Science

Peter leads PhysAI Lab, developing physical simulators at the intersection of computer graphics, machine learning, scientific computing, and robotics. His research connects efficient computation with applications in science, engineering, and 3D content creation.
